## Deep-review decisions

- `telephony-computing-networking` resolves to Laptops from its descendant path and titles; `computer-accessory-component` is dominated by keyboards and replacement components rather than complete devices.
- `point-sale-retail-technology` contains printers, scanners, copiers, and label printers. `office-accessory` is a Xiaomi Mi Router 4C and belongs in Networking.
- `telecom-ict-product` mixes airtime and mobile accessories and moves to the deliberately broad Mobile Services & Accessories family.
- Laptop batteries, fans, and replacement screens are parts. General flash disks, memory cards, and external hard drives are Computing storage.
- Phones and Tablets are separate canonical buttons. `phone-tablet` and `computer-tablet` remain visibly marked compatibility shelves until their products are reassigned; iPhones and iPads are separate planned buttons.
- `landline-phone-accessory` contains both devices and accessories and therefore stays broad. Tablet covers, MagSafe wallets, SmartTags, and SIM trays are separated by product type.
- `interactive-display` is dominated by replacement touch displays with some conference displays and moves to Computing. `tv-entertainment` contains earbuds and joins Headphones & Earbuds.
- The so-called CCTV shelf publishes a Cameras child and representative body/action cameras, so it remains broad Cameras & Surveillance rather than being narrowed to CCTV.
- Smartwatches remain Wearables and ordinary watches remain Fashion. Pass 5 corrected `watch-3bab17` after its complete current capture showed only Samsung/Huawei wearables; TP-Link, Razer Blade, Mac, and X380 do not become brand/model families.
