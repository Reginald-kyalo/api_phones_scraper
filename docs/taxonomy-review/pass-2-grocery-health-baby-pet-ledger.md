## Representative evidence behind corrections

- `food-cupboard` contains rice, pulses, oils, snacks, and other shelf-stable foods, so it remains the broad Pantry entry while narrower pantry families remain available.
- `cooking` contains vinegar, wasabi, and miso; it joins Sauces & Condiments. `infusion-de4b3b` contains flavoured tea bags and joins Tea, Coffee & Hot Drinks.
- `tetra-one-litre-b521c1` and `can-a6b58d` contain juice and canned soda; `long-life-62b199` contains shelf-stable milk. These are product formats, not categories, and map to drinks or dairy.
- `healthy-snack-beverage` contains dried fruit and nuts and now has the shopper-facing Nuts, Seeds & Dried Fruit intent; `cafe-naiva` contains prepared foods; `ice-cream-dessert` contains gelato and frozen desserts.
- `paper-plastic` contains basins, buckets, drums, and soap dishes and moves to Plastic Housewares. `house-hold-d1c761` is dishwashing liquid and moves to Dishwashing.
- `cistern-block`, bar-soap tablets, serviettes, scouring powder, and scouring pads now map to their evidenced cleaning types rather than an unresolved bucket. The broad `cleaning-household` and `soap-detergent` shelves remain broad because their samples mix several cleaning product types.
- `fire-lighter-match` and `match-box-1dcac5` are cooking/fire-lighting supplies and move to Kitchen & Dining, separate from appliances and cookware.
- `baking-flour-4d6f18` contains cupcake moulds and cases, not flour; `coconut-powder` contains baby powder; `wipe` is dominated by baby wipes; and `cold-drink` contains a reusable tea bag. Each moves according to its products.
- `soya` and `malt-based-ad3e2a` contain powdered drinks, while `sphaghetti-6992a0` contains prepared bolognese. Product type wins over the ambiguous source label.
- `beauty-personal-care`, `spray-cc8d75`, and `baby-kid` have stable broad shopper intents; direct products remain reachable through Personal Care, Fragrance, and Baby Essentials.

## Compatibility checks

- Baby formula, toiletries, feeding, and maternity products remain under Baby.
- Grocery skincare, shaving, first-aid, and oral-care products move to Health & Beauty.
- Retail pet goods move to Pet Supplies. Pass 6 corrected `pet` to Pet Care and `poultry` to grocery meat after complete current captures showed retail supplies and fresh/frozen food rather than live-animal listings.
- Kenyan terms including `omena` and `mandazi` retain their unchanged searchable legacy slugs.
- Every captured shelf in the three reviewed source departments has exactly one explicit disposition; no generated fixture is edited.
